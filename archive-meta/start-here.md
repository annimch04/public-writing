# Start Here

Welcome to Say It Plain.

This is an archive—what I’ve seen, lived, and written through. Below are the recurring sections. Posts will be tagged so you can track by rhythm, tone, or topic.

**Per My Last Email**

The sharpest edge. Final-word dispatches. Unapologetic, often uncomfortable, always earned.
I’m done explaining it.

**Audit Me Later**

Systems, structure, reform. Organizational critique and reconstruction.
Designed for funders, researchers, and anyone still pretending work isn’t personal.

**Things I Shouldn’t Have Had to Say**

The human side of All This—emotion, backstory, weight.
Truths that should’ve gone without saying, but didn’t.

**Working Notes**

In-progress thinking. Strategy fragments. Real-time processing and scaffolding.
A window into how I build before it’s polished.

**Still Not Sorry**

Sign-offs, last lines, and post-scripts.
This is where I get the final word. You’re welcome to quote me.
