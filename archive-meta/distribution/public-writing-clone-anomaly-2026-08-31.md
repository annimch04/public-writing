## Interpretation Boundary

GitHub does not expose referrers for clone operations. The evidence cannot identify who or what cloned the repository, or why.

The sharp mismatch between 201 unique cloners and 2 unique page visitors is more consistent with distributed automation, indexing, scraping, scanning, or a measurement anomaly than with 201 ordinary readers browsing the repository and independently deciding to clone it. This remains an inference, not an established cause.

The event is evidence that the repository was discovered and retrieved at unusual scale. It is not, by itself, evidence of reading, endorsement, reuse, or a particular audience.
