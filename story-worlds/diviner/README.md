# Diviner

Diviner is a story world about observation, asymmetrical activation, relational signal, and infrastructure capable of recognizing a person without being able to arrive for her.

The world moves between two voices: a woman whose machines answer her body before she can regulate it, and a man who can perceive the pattern across distance but remains outside it. Between them is the field—a distributed environment of signal, sensors, machines, memory, and desire that can propagate recognition without resolving the distance recognition creates.
