# A Snake Named Jake

_Small Stories for the Child in All of Us — Entry 03_

A snake named Jake, a mischievous one,  
Escaped from his cage in search of some fun.  
He slithered through the house, with no time to waste—  
A secret adventure was waiting to taste.

With a flick of his tongue, he explored the hall,  
Curtains for climbing and paintings on the wall.  
In the bathroom, he peered in the toilet bowl,  
Then turned himself round. That was not his goal.

Next came the bedroom, where he made his bed.  
In a nest of blankets, he hid his head.  
But morning crept close. Jake knew his fate:  
Back to the cage before it was too late.

With a chuckle and hiss, he slithered on back—  
No footsteps behind him, no trace of his track.  
He curled up content, as still as could be,  
With a whole house of secrets for company.
