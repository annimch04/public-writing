Status: Active register 0.1  
Established: August 26, 2026  
Maintained by: Anni McHenry  

## Purpose

This register records public work that crosses the boundary between Anni McHenry's personal authorship corpus and Fieldlight Institute's institutional programs, standards, and implementations.

The repositories do not have to contain identical files. They do have to state which authority each copy preserves.

- `annimch04/public-writing` preserves Anni McHenry's canonical authorship sources.
- Fieldlight Institute repositories preserve institutionally adopted theories, constitutional artifacts, research records, protocols, and implementation specifications.
- `fieldlight.com` provides public reading surfaces. A reading surface is a representation, not the source of authorship or institutional authority.

## Routing Rules

1. An Anni-authored public work belongs in `public-writing`, even when an Institute program adopts or operationalizes it.
2. An Institute copy of an authored work must identify the canonical authorship source and its institutional status.
3. Constitutional, program, and implementation artifacts proposed by Fieldlight Institute may remain Institute-only.
4. An Institute-only artifact is registered here when it materially relates to Anni's public corpus; registration does not convert it into personal writing.
5. Two full copies may exist only when their roles are explicit. Silent duplication is not a preservation strategy.
6. Every public reading surface should resolve to the relevant authorial source, institutional source, or both.

## Maintenance Rule

When a new Fieldlight Institute work becomes public, answer three questions before publication is considered complete:

1. Who or what holds the authorship authority?
2. Which repository holds the institutional authority?
3. Which public surface represents the work to readers?

If any answer is unknown, register the gap rather than allowing repository location to answer it by accident.
