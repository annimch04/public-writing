## Status And Lineage

The language belongs to the early Fieldlight vocabulary: signal, resonance, protected zones, authorship boundaries, and aligned entry. It is preserved without retroactive rewriting.

The protocol established several commitments that became more explicit in later Fieldlight work:

- visibility does not transfer authorship;
- entry is not permission to extract, reshape, or claim;
- private systems remain protected unless access is expressly granted;
- collaboration requires named presence and intent;
- engagement may be declined or revoked.

The current institutional successor is the [Fieldlight Institute Collaboration Gate](https://github.com/fieldlight-institute/governance-protocols/blob/main/COLLABORATION-GATE.md). The successor translates the original boundaries into public, operational terms for collaboration with the Institute.

This artifact is historical. It is not a current invitation, contact protocol, access grant, or license.
