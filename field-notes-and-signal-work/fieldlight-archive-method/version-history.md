# Version History

## Version 1.0 amendment — August 21, 2026

Adds the cross-surface completeness rule without changing identifier semantics or the source-versus-representation model:

- no single preservation surface should be presumed complete;
- completeness must be stated against a declared set of inspected surfaces and dated source states;
- the operational protocol now includes a preservation-surface register and surface-aware quality checks;
- an April 23–24, 2025 locally cached conversation absent from all three official exports is recorded as the implementation finding that required the amendment.

The cache finding establishes local historical survival outside the export union. It does not establish why the conversation was absent, and it does not make the local cache itself complete or authoritative.

## 1.0 — August 2026

First stable external specification.

Introduces:

- the source-versus-representation model;
- the ten-stage path from scope through intentional publication;
- declared orientation coverage;
- stable, never-recycled identifiers;
- claim-level evidence basis and confidence;
- bounded evidence packets;
- human–AI responsibility boundaries;
- correction as a first-class archival record;
- separate private-analysis and public-release authority;
- templates and a redacted worked example;
- a public companion workbook with reusable registers, aggregate field-test evidence, 71 public-work records, corrections, research questions, and a release gate;
- CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licensing for the method package, with explicit exclusions for the private archive and referenced works.

The private working method used to develop this release carried internal revisions as new domains exposed missing rules. Those internal numbers are implementation history, not earlier public releases. Public Version 1.0 is the first version intended for external use.

## Compatibility rule

Minor versions may add fields, examples, or optional checks without changing the meaning of existing records. A major version is required if identifier semantics, evidentiary categories, privacy authority, or the source-versus-representation model changes incompatibly.
