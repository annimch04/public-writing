# Public Workbook Release Review

## Proposed derivative

**Title:** *Fieldlight Archive Method — Public Workbook*  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared:** 2026-08-13  
**Authority:** Anni McHenry authorized creation of a public derivative and requested that the public-work catalog be included.  
**License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for the method, schema, workbook design, and controlled compilation elements; linked works retain their separately recorded rights status.

## Decision

Publish the derived workbook. Do not publish the private `SFA-inventory.xlsx` master.

The private master is a custody and analysis instrument, not a public artifact. A sheet-by-sheet audit found 1,634 local-path references, 1,311 appearances of the private designation, 356 appearances of the restricted designation, and eight security- or credential-related terms. Counts include repeated entries and labels, but they establish that redacting a few cells would not create a reliable public release.

## What the derivative retains

- the source-versus-representation model;
- the stable identifier standard;
- blank source, representation, connection, correction, and release-review tables;
- aggregate field-test evidence from physical, photographic, academic, born-digital, public, and whole-corpus work;
- three real method corrections, stated without private source content;
- the three creator-supplied research questions and Stanford-application flag;
- 71 public-work, series, representation, and repository records with canonical public links, provenance anchors, and recorded rights status.

## What the derivative excludes

- all local filesystem paths;
- private and restricted source rows;
- captures and file manifests tied to private evidence;
- private cross-source connection claims;
- family, health, correspondence, credential, contact, and precise-location context;
- quotations, transcriptions, and images from private sources;
- private archive cross-references from the public-work register.

## Verification

The exported workbook was reopened from disk, every sheet was rendered for visual inspection, and the saved file was scanned for spreadsheet formula errors. No formula errors were found. The privacy scan found no local paths, private source identifiers, named private subjects, access tokens, or passwords. Its only security-term match was the word “credential” in the release checklist question instructing users to remove such details.

## Correction and withdrawal path

Errors in the public workbook should be corrected in the public package and recorded in the version history. A rights, privacy, or safety problem can justify immediate withdrawal while the private master remains preserved and unchanged.
